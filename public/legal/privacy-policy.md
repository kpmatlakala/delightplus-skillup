# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 15 April 2026

CET Connect Portal ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website cet-connect-portal.netlify.app, use our services, or interact with our platform.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Learner Data:** Name, ID number, contact details, institution, department (collected during self-registration or assessment)
- **Assessment Data:** Quiz answers, summative assessment submissions, progress tracking
- **Technical Data:** IP address, browser type, device info, usage patterns (via analytics)

### Non-Personal Information
- Aggregated usage statistics
- Module completion rates

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- Deliver educational content and track learner progress
- Process assessments and generate certificates
- Communicate assessment results and programme updates
- Improve platform functionality
- Comply with SAQA accreditation requirements

## 3. Legal Basis & Compliance
- **POPIA (South Africa):** Personal Information processed lawfully for legitimate educational purposes
- **Supabase Hosting:** EU-based with GDPR compliance; data encrypted at rest/transit
- **Retention:** Learner data kept 5 years post-programme completion (SAQA requirement)

## 4. Data Sharing
We **do not** sell your data. Sharing limited to:
- DSA administrators for assessment marking
- SAQA/SETA for accreditation verification
- Service providers (Supabase, Netlify) under strict agreements

## 5. Your Rights
- **Access:** Request your data anytime
- **Rectification:** Update incorrect information  
- **Erasure:** Request deletion (subject to SAQA retention)
- **Objection:** Opt-out of non-essential processing

Contact: privacy@cetconnect.academy

## 6. Security
- HTTPS encryption
- Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS)
- Assessment submissions logged with timestamps

## 7. Cookies
- Session cookies for authentication
- Analytics (opt-out available)

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
