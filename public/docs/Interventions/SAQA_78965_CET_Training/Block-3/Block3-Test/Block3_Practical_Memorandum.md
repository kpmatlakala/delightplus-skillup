## Individual Accountability Rules

Award individual evidence marks only when ALL are present:
- Learner completed personal contribution entries for each station.
- Learner declaration is signed.
- Assessor observed contribution or learner can explain submitted work.

**Recommended consequence for missing evidence:**
- Missing one station personal log: cap final mark at 75% until remediation.
- Missing declaration: withhold final result pending declaration.
