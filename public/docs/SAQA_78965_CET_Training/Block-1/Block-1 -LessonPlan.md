# **LESSON PLAN — 14924: Information Systems Analysis**

**Module:** ITSD-14924 | Block 1 | Day 1  
**Training Intervention:** CET Lecturers Systems Development Training

## **Lesson Title - Systems Analysis Foundations: SDLC, Roles and Techniques**

## **Lesson Objectives**

By the end of this session, learners will be able to:

1.  Explain the role of information systems analysis within the Software Development Life Cycle (Session 1).
2.  Describe the key responsibilities of an information systems analyst (Session 1).
3.  Identify and explain common information-gathering techniques (Session 1).
4.  Describe industry-standard systems analysis techniques (Session 2).  
    

## **Lesson Content**

### **Session 1: Introduction to Information Systems Analysis**

-   SDLC Phases: Investigation → Analysis → Design → Development → Implementation → Maintenance  
    
-   Roles of the Systems Analyst:
    -   Investigating business problems
    -   Identifying and recommending solutions
    -   Planning system improvements
    -   Coordinating development activities  
        
-   Information-Gathering Techniques:
    -   Interviews
    -   Questionnaires
    -   Observation
    -   Site visits
    -   Review of documentation and forms  
        
-   Distinguishing Systems Analysis from Requirements Analysis  
    

### **Session 2: Systems Analysis Techniques**

-   Data Flow Diagrams (DFDs)
-   Decision Trees and Decision Tables
-   Computer-Aided Software Engineering (CASE) tools
-   Data Structures and Business Process Modelling  
    

## 

## **Facilitator Activities**

-   Icebreaker (15 min): Share a technology challenge experienced in your department.
-   Mini-lecture (20 min): SDLC overview and the analyst’s role in a CET context.
-   Group Activity: Develop a stakeholder map for a student project tracking system.
-   Paired Exercise: Select the most appropriate information-gathering technique for a given scenario.
-   Provide Systems Analysis Quick-Start Toolkit (DFD templates, stakeholder templates).
-   Peer Review: Exchange and critique selected analysis techniques.  
    

## **Learner Activities**

-   Activity 1 (5 marks): Differentiate between Systems Analysis and Requirements Analysis.
-   Activity 2 (9 marks): Describe the functions of an Information Systems Analyst.
-   Activity 3 (12 marks): Explain information-gathering techniques.
-   Activity 4 (15 marks): Describe systems analysis techniques (DFDs, Decision Tables, CASE).
-   Group Task: Develop a stakeholder map for a CET lab booking system.  
    

## **Resources**

-   SDLC flowchart (printed)
-   Stakeholder mapping templates
-   Information-gathering checklist
-   Flipchart paper and markers
-   Bilingual glossary (Sepedi/Tshivenda key terms)
-   Printed backup materials (offline contingency)
-   Facilitator and Learner Guides (SAQA 14924)  
    

## **Assessment (Formative)**

-   Observation checklist (participation and stakeholder identification)
-   Completed worksheets (Activities 1–4)
-   Peer feedback session
-   Exit reflection ticket
-   Evidence aligned to SAQA 14924 summative requirements  
    

**Duration:** 300 minutes (5 hours)  
**Status:** Active

# **LESSON PLAN — 14920: Team Collaboration and Problem Solving**

**Module:** ITSD-14920 | Block 1 | Day 2  
**Training Intervention:** CET Lecturers Systems Development Training

## **Lesson Title - Teamwork Foundations: Collaboration, Roles and Problem Solving**

## **Lesson Objectives**

By the end of this session, learners will be able to:

1.  Identify common challenges faced by teams (Session 1).
2.  Explain how individuals can contribute effectively to team problem-solving (Session 1).
3.  Describe the benefits of teamwork (Session 2).
4.  Propose strategies to build a collaborative team environment (Session 2).

## **Lesson Content**

### **Session 1: Contributing to Team Problem Solving**

-   Common Team Challenges:
    -   Unclear goals
    -   Role confusion
    -   Poor communication
    -   Conflict
    -   Leadership issues  
        
-   Problem-Solving Process:  
    Plan → Build Team → Define Problem → Implement Solution → Verify → Prevent Recurrence  
    
-   Nominal Group Technique (NGT) for structured idea generation  
    

### **Session 2: Effective Team Functioning**

-   Benefits of Teamwork: improved reliability, communication, participation, cooperation
-   Characteristics of Effective Team Members: flexibility, accountability, respect, initiative
-   Building Collaboration: shared purpose, trust, defined roles, open communication  
    

## **Facilitator Activities**

-   Icebreaker: Share a positive and challenging team experience.
-   Mini-lecture: Team challenges and the NGT process.
-   Group Task: Draft a team charter for capstone projects.
-   Paired Exercise: Identify team challenges and propose solutions.
-   Provide Team Quick-Start Toolkit (Charter template, NGT guide).
-   Peer Review: Evaluate team charters for clarity and role definition.  
    

## **Learner Activities**

Activities 1–6 covering team challenges, problem-solving contributions, NGT suitability, teamwork benefits, effective team qualities, and collaborative strategies.  
Group Task: Develop and present a Team Charter with assigned roles.

## **Resources**

-   Team charter templates
-   NGT guide
-   Problem-solving checklist
-   Bilingual glossary
-   Printed offline backup materials
-   Facilitator and Learner Guides (SAQA 14920)  
    

## **Assessment (Formative)**
