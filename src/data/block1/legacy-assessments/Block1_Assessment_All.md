## Day 1 — Module 14924: Information Systems Analysis

📝 **Summative Test (60% MCQ + 40% Short-Answer)**
**Section A: MCQ (60%)**
- Identify the correct phase of SDLC for requirements gathering
- Select the appropriate information-gathering technique for a scenario
- Match analysis techniques (DFD, Decision Table) to their definitions
- Identify the role of a systems analyst from a list of functions
**Section B: Structured Short-Answer (40%)**
- Differentiate between Systems Analysis and Requirements Analysis (bullet points)
- List 3 functions of an Information Systems Analyst in a CET context
- Complete a table matching gathering techniques to their advantages/disadvantages
- Label a diagram of a simple DFD with correct symbols


🛠️ **Practical Task (PoE Evidence)**
- Group Task: Develop a stakeholder map for a CET lab booking system (focus is on knowledge of group/team participation, not execution of a group project)
- Submission: Printed A3 map + group contribution log
- Sign-Off: Facilitator observes participation + signs completion sheet

📖 **Workbook Activities (Formative)**
- Complete SDLC flowchart (printed template)
- Fill in stakeholder mapping templates
- Use information-gathering checklist during group work
