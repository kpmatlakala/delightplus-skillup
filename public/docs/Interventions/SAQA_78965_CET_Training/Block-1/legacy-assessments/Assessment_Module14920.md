# Module 14920 Assessment Plan

## Workbook (Formative)

### Session 1: Participate in Groups/Teams
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identifies common challenges faced by teams
- Explains how individuals contribute to team problem-solving
- Describes the benefits of teamwork
- Proposes strategies to build a collaborative team environment

**Activities:**

## Extra Assessment Items (Simple English)

- Name one rule that helps a team work well together.
- Your team is not talking to each other. What are two things you can do to fix this?
- Write one sentence about how your team solves problems.
- After a group task, say one good thing you did as a team member.

## Quiz Bank (Optional)

- Which of these is a common team problem?
- What is the main benefit of using the Nominal Group Technique?
- Which quality is important for teamwork?
- What is the main difference between a group and a team?
