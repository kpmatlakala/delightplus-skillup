# Module 14924 Assessment Plan

## Workbook (Formative)

### Session 1: Describe Information Systems Analysis
**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identifies the position of information systems analysis in the SDLC
- Explains the purpose of information systems analysis
- Outlines functions of the information systems analyst
- Outlines information gathering techniques used by information systems analysts
- Explains different systems analysis techniques used in the industry

**Activities:**
| Activity | Question Description | Mark |
|----------|---------------------|------|
| 1        | Distinguish between Systems Analysis and Requirements Analysis | 5    |

## Extra Assessment Items (Simple English)

- Why is it important to do analysis before building a system?
- Change this sentence into a clear requirement: "The system must be fast and easy to use."
- Draw a simple diagram showing how students register for school. Label the people and the steps.
- List two reasons why a school might choose Agile instead of Waterfall for a new system.
