# Using the Learner Portal

> Sample guide. Replace with the published PDF once finalised.

## 1. Signing in
Use the email address registered with your facilitator. If you've forgotten your
password, use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen.

## 2. Your dashboard
- **Current mission** — the module you're actively working through.
- **Quick Access** — unread messages and the next scheduled check-in.
- **Ops Feed** — announcements from your facilitator.

## 3. Working through a module
Each module follows the same flow:
1. Read the **Knowledge** guide.
2. Complete the **Practical** workbook activities.
3. Attempt the **Formative** quiz.
4. Submit the **Summative** assessment when your facilitator unlocks it with an OTP.

## 4. Communications
Use the **Updates** tab for announcements and the **Messages** tab to talk to
your facilitator or site coordinator.

## 5. Portfolio of Evidence (PoE)
All completed workbooks, assessments and supporting evidence are gathered in the
**PoE** area. Submit your PoE before the moderation date listed on your schedule.
